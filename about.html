Our Story & Leadership

About The Daily Lens

Independent. Informative. Dedicated to the People of Malawi.

Founded and edited by Malawian digital journalists Verena Likupe and Charity Ndhlovu as part of the Bachelor of Arts in Digital Journalism (BDJOU2), The DailyLens is an independent digital news publishing platform based in Lilongwe. We are committed to rigorous, fearless, and community-centred reporting that illuminates national progress, holds institutions accountable, and amplifies the voices of Malawians shaping the nation’s future.

Editorial Leadership & Founders

The DailyLens was conceptualized and developed collaboratively by two student journalists in the Bachelor of Arts in Digital Journalism (BDJOU2) program, combining rigorous investigative reporting with modern digital publishing architecture.

Co-Founder & Senior Investigative Reporter

Verena Likupe

Verena Likupe is a dynamic Malawian digital journalist specializing in civic accountability, technology policy, and institutional governance. In her reporting for The DailyLens, Verena leads investigations into district council decentralization, youth parliamentary civic engagement, and higher education digital access in Malawi.

“Digital journalism provides Malawians with an unprecedented tool to bridge regional divides. Our goal with The DailyLens is to ensure every story adheres to strict verification, dignified framing, and public interest transparency,” notes Verena Likupe.

Desk: Civic Affairs & Technology verena@thedailylens.com
Co-Founder & Managing Editor

Charity Ndhlovu

Charity Ndhlovu is a dedicated multimedia journalist, field producer, and documentary photographer whose work has documented grassroots development, environmental conservation, and renewable energy transformation across Malawi. From smallholder irrigation in Dowa to engineering hubs at MUBAS and MUST, Charity brings grounded empathy and factual rigor.

“Journalism must belong to the communities it covers. In a rapidly transforming Malawi, we ensure that whether an innovation emerges from a workshop in Blantyre or a village garden in Dedza, it receives national prominence,” says Charity Ndhlovu.

Our Editorial Pillars

Investigative Rigour

We prioritize thorough, on-the-ground investigation over sensationalism, holding government bodies, corporations, and civil society to high standards of transparency.

Championing Malawian Innovation

We actively showcase Malawian engineers, student inventors, agronomists, and startups building homegrown solutions for local challenges.

National & District Inclusivity

Our reporters travel beyond the commercial hubs of Lilongwe and Blantyre to document stories across Northern, Central, and Southern Malawi districts.

Ethical Standards & Independence

We maintain strict editorial independence, adhering to the Media Council of Malawi code of conduct and verifying facts before publication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Verena Likupe and Charity Ndhlovu lead The DailyLens editorial team. Together, they commission, report, edit, and publish public-interest stories that matter to communities across Malawi.

We cover the people, decisions, and ideas shaping daily life in Malawi. Our reporting spans public affairs, business, technology, sport, the environment, education, and local community news, with a focus on clear context and useful reporting.

Every story is reviewed before publication. Reporters seek confirmation from the people and institutions involved, check supporting records where available, and give readers the context needed to understand why a story matters.
