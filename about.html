Our Story & Leadership

About The Daily Lens

Independent. Informative. Dedicated to the People of Malawi.

Founded and led by Malawian investigative journalist Charity Ndhlovu, The DailyLens is an independent digital news organization based in Lilongwe. We are committed to rigorous, fearless, and community-centered reporting that illuminates progress, holds institutions accountable, and amplifies the voices of Malawians shaping the nation’s future.

Meet the Editor: Charity Ndhlovu

Charity Ndhlovu is a dedicated Malawian multimedia journalist, field producer, and documentary photographer whose work has documented grassroots development, environmental conservation, and technological transformation across all regions of Malawi. From covering smallholder farmer irrigation schemes in Dowa and Mchinji to investigating higher education research hubs at MUBAS and MUST, Charity brings grounded empathy and meticulous fact-checking to every assignment.

“Our vision with The DailyLens is straightforward: journalism must belong to the communities it covers. In a rapidly digitizing Malawi, we ensure that whether an innovation emerges from a workshop in Blantyre or a village garden in Dedza, it receives fair, constructive, and dignified national coverage,” says Charity Ndhlovu.

Our Editorial Pillars

Investigative Rigour

We prioritize thorough, on-the-ground investigation over sensationalism, holding government bodies, corporations, and civil society to high standards of transparency.

Championing Malawian Innovation

We actively showcase Malawian engineers, student inventors, agronomists, and startups building homegrown solutions for local challenges.

National & District Inclusivity

Our reporters travel beyond the commercial hubs of Lilongwe and Blantyre to document stories across Northern, Central, and Southern Malawi districts.

Ethical Standards & Independence

We maintain strict editorial independence, adhering to the Media Council of Malawi code of conduct and verifying facts before publication.
